About the Game

Neyasnoe is a walk you take at night, through the places that dont't ask anything of you. There's no quest log, no meter to fill, no way to lose — just streets that keep unfolding for as long as you're willing to stay in them.

Light a cigarette. Order one more drink. Talk to whoever's still awake at this hour, or keep walking past them. Somewhere in the street light you'll run into a stray line of verse — unfinished, unexpected, worth pausing for.

It's less a game than a simulator of escapism.

Built with Godot 3.4.2, with models made in Blender 2.83 and Steam integration through Steamworks SDK 1.53a.
